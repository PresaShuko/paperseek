# Your account

An online account keeps your searches, analyses, reviews, saved keys, and credits together. The Community edition you install yourself does not need a PaperSeek account.

## Create an account

1. Click **Not signed in** at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Create account**.
2. Enter your email address and send the verification code.
3. Enter the six-digit code from the email.
4. Set and confirm a password.

Use at least eight characters, and follow any further requirements the form shows.

## Sign in

Sign in with your email and password, with a one-time email code, or with GitHub, Hugging Face, or ModelScope when they are offered. Signing in with a provider only signs you in to PaperSeek; it does not let PaperSeek use that provider's models.

If you use more than one sign-in method, check the email and account ID in **Settings › User** before continuing. Two different email addresses do not share history.

## Settings › User

Open it from the account menu (**Account & credits**) or from **Settings**.

- **Account** shows your email and account ID. The ID is masked; use **Show** or **Copy** when contacting support. It is not an API key.
- **Credits** shows today's remaining credits, the refill time, and what each task uses. See [Credits](index.md#credits).
- **Sign-in methods** links or unlinks GitHub, Hugging Face, and ModelScope, and sets a password for an account created without one. Unlinking keeps your history.
- **Data management**:
  - **Clear keys** deletes every model connection and data-source key saved to your account. Type `clear saved keys` to confirm. Submitted tasks are not affected.
  - **Clear history** deletes all searches, analyses, and reviews with their exported files; type `clear` to confirm. Running tasks are stopped first. Credit records are kept. This cannot be undone.

Signing out does not stop tasks that are already running.

## Email or password problems

- Check spam and the spelling of your email address.
- Use the most recent code; requesting a new code can replace the earlier one.
- If you forgot your password, choose **Email code** in the sign-in dialog. Signing in with a code does not change your password.
- If saving the password fails, note the error and the time and try again. Never send your password or verification code to anyone, including support.

A temporary error during registration is no reason to create several accounts. See [Troubleshooting](../reference/troubleshooting.md).
