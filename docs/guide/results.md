# Results and history

Inspect your reading list, select what matters, and export it into your own research workflow.

## Three independent histories

Choose a module in the left sidebar to see its **Search history**, **Analyses**, or **Reviews**. Search runs, analyses, and reviews are saved separately. Use the magnifying glass beside the history heading to search records, or **All** to open the full list and its record menus for rename, export, and delete. See [workspace and history](workspace.md).

## Read a paper card

Results can include titles, authors, years, venues, abstracts, source links, identifiers, citation information, and relevance explanations. Availability depends on the source.

Relevance measures the match to **your question**. It is not a study-quality score or permission to cite a paper without reading it.

## Filter, sort, and select

Result controls search the returned set, apply available filters, and change the order. They do not issue a fresh external search.

Select individual papers for a smaller export or a review, and check the selected count before continuing. **Select loaded** applies only to the currently loaded papers. Review creation requires 2–100 selected papers. Switching modules preserves the selection during this session.

## Export

| Format | Use |
| --- | --- |
| CSV | Spreadsheets and manual screening |
| JSON | Structured downstream processing |
| Selected CSV / JSON | Only the selected papers |

The **Export** menu groups complete and selected CSV/JSON downloads. A complete export covers the stored results, not just the visible page. Original-record links take you to the source; PaperSeek does not bulk-download paywalled text.

[Review exports](reviews.md) use Markdown, Word, and BibTeX.

## Reopen history

Community history belongs to the local database. Online history belongs to your account. Signing in after an anonymous search does not backfill it.

A saved run retains its actual settings. Reopening it does not replace its model or budget with current defaults; older unrecorded fields remain unknown.

You can delete finished history items after exporting anything you need. Active tasks must end first.

## Start a review

Select at least two papers with useful abstracts, or create a review through **Reviews**. Follow the [outline and writing guide](reviews.md).
