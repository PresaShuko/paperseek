# Explore citation connections

See the research neighborhood around a search, rather than isolated papers.

## Get a map

Choose **OpenAlex** and enable citation expansion before searching. References and citing papers are explored within the chosen budget.

Open **Citation evidence** from the results, or **Reviews → Citation evidence → Paper relations**. An empty map is expected when no citation data was collected; changing tabs does not retroactively run expansion.

## Read the graph

Nodes represent papers. Arrows point from the citing paper to the cited paper.

The legend distinguishes result, seed, and forward/backward expansion roles. These describe how a paper entered the graph, not its quality.

Select a node to inspect its paper and available source link. Adjust the view with graph controls when the network is dense.

## Understand the limits

This is a bounded exploration, not a complete field-wide citation network. Source coverage, missing identifiers, and updates affect the connections.

Citation counts are not relevance scores: a recent paper may be relevant with few citations.

Return to [Results](results.md) to read, select, or export.
